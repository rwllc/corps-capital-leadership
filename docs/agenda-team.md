## EOS: THE FRAMEWORK

EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) is built on six components. Each one strengthens the others.

| Component | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Gets everyone on the same page — where you're going and how you'll get there |
| **People** | The right people in the right seats, evaluated with clear criteria |
| **Data** | A handful of numbers that tell you how the business is running — every week |
| **Issues** | A systematic way to surface, prioritize, and solve problems permanently |
| **Process** | The core ways the business works — documented and followed by all |
| **Traction** | Discipline and accountability — turning vision into execution, every 90 days |

The primary tool is the **V/TO (Vision/Traction Organizer)** — a two-page document that captures the entire vision and 90-day plan for the company on a single sheet.

**2:15 – 3:30 | ISSUES: FULL TEAM IDS SESSION**
*Bob facilitates using the Issues Solving Track*

The Issues Solving Track — **Identify, Discuss, Solve** — is how EOS teams make decisions without circular conversation.

- **Identify:** What is the *real* issue? (Not just the symptom)
- **Discuss:** Everyone speaks once. No recycling arguments.
- **Solve:** Every issue ends with a decision, a to-do, and an owner — or it goes on the long-term Issues List

We work through the company's highest-priority issues together.
